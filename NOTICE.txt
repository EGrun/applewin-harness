ROM images
----------
AppleWinHarness.exe contains Apple II ROM images (the Apple II, II+ and IIe
system, video and peripheral card ROMs) compiled in from AppleWin's resources
(external/AppleWin/resource). They are included on the same terms as AppleWin's
own releases.

Disk images
-----------
No disk images are included in this repository or in the release bundle. A
DOS 3.3 System Master disk image can be found in the AppleWin repository's
bin/ directory (https://github.com/AppleWin/AppleWin/tree/master/bin).
