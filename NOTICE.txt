ROM images
----------
AppleWinHarness.exe contains Apple II ROM images (the Apple II, II+ and IIe
system, video and peripheral card ROMs, and other firmware) compiled in from
AppleWin's resources (external/AppleWin/resource). These images are not
covered by the GPL or the MIT license: they are the copyright of Apple Inc.
and other third parties, and we know of no license that covers them. They are
redistributed here only in the way AppleWin has long redistributed them.
Whether that is permitted for you is your own responsibility; to avoid
carrying them, build from source and do not distribute the exe.

Disk images
-----------
No disk images are included in this repository's own files or in the release
bundle. A DOS 3.3 System Master disk image (the copyright of Apple Inc., not
covered by these licenses) can be found in the AppleWin repository's bin/
directory (https://github.com/AppleWin/AppleWin/tree/master/bin). Checking out
the external/AppleWin submodule also fetches the images AppleWin keeps there.
