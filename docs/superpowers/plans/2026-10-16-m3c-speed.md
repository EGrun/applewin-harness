# M3 Part C — Speed Controls Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the operator API offers AppleWin's speed controls exactly: speed slider, clock multiplier, enhanced disk speed, the Scroll Lock override. Realtime pacing follows them, with AppleWin's full-speed rules. Stepped results never change.

**Architecture:**
- `Emulator` gains a small speed API that sets AppleWin's own globals through AppleWin's functions.
- The realtime step primitive copies `ContinueExecution`'s full-speed decision and video handling.
- The session exposes `set_speed`, `get_speed` and `create_session.speed`, and caps `frame_ready` at one per 16 ms of wall time (about 62.5/s) while at full speed.
- **Tests come first (Task 1).**

**Tech Stack:** C++20, MSVC v143, doctest, Python 3 standard library.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/m3.md` Part C and `docs/api_spec.md` Speed, both binding. D9 and D11 too.

## Global Constraints
- **Values.** `machine_speed` is 0–40, `"authentic"` (= 10) or `"max"` (= 40). `clock_multiplier` is any finite number above 0; sending it together with `machine_speed` gives `-32602`. Both `enhance_disk_speed` (default true) and `full_speed_override` (default false) are booleans.
- **AppleWin calls.**
  - `machine_speed` sets `g_dwSpeed`, then calls `SetCurrentCLK6502()`.
  - `clock_multiplier` calls `UseClockMultiplier(x)`.
  - `enhance_disk_speed` calls `GetCardMgr().GetDisk2CardMgr().SetEnhanceDisk(b)`.
- **Full speed** is `g_dwSpeed == SPEED_MAX || override || (Disk2CardMgr.IsConditionForFullSpeed() && !Spkr_IsActive() && !MockingboardCardMgr.IsActiveToPreventFullSpeed())`. It applies only in realtime runs.
  - It runs unpaced steps with `CpuExecute(cycles, false)`.
  - Video goes through AppleWin's `VideoRedrawScreenDuringFullSpeed(0, true)` on entry, then at most every 16 ms of wall time, and `VideoRedrawScreenAfterFullSpeed(g_dwCyclesThisFrame)` on exit.
  - `frame_ready` is capped at one per 16 ms of wall time (about 62.5/s).
- **Stepped mode** never uses full speed, and always renders video with `CpuExecute(cycles, true)`.
- **`get_speed`** fields are exactly as `api_spec.md` lists them. `effective_clock_hz` is `g_fCurrentCLK6502`, or null at 40. `base_clock_hz` is `CLK_6502_NTSC`. `video_refresh` is 60. `full_speed_reasons` lists `"machine_speed_max"`, `"override"` and `"disk_motor"`, in that order.
- **`create_session.speed`** accepts the same fields plus `video_refresh: 60` only; 50 gives `-32006`.
- **Speed is a session setting,** and `restore_checkpoint` (M4) won't change it.
- **Golden hashes are unchanged.** The M2 golden runs in stepped mode, at whatever speed is set.
- **Rate measurements** go in doctest suite `realtime` (C++) or are marked as timing tests (Python). The tolerance is ±2% over 3 s after a 1 s settle, headless only.

2. **The disk-motor full-speed rule fires while the speaker is active.** Expected: AppleWin's rule; full speed only with the speaker and Mockingboard silent. *(Task 1 C++ test: boot DOS at speed 10 in realtime; `full_speed_reasons` contains `"disk_motor"` during boot and is empty once at the prompt. 3. **The pacer drifts after a speed change mid-run.** Expected: the new rate is reached within one 1 ms tick budget. The `BurstBudget` is rebuilt whenever `g_fCurrentCLK6502` changes. *(Task 1 test: speed 10 → 20 mid-run; the rate over the next 3 s is within ±2% of the 20-speed rate.)*
4. **Agent audio changes with speed.** Expected: a tone keeps its pitch at speeds 10 and 20 (capture is in emulated time). *(Part B's skipped `realtime` test is unskipped here.)*
5. **`clock_multiplier` round-trip.** Expected: for 0.3, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.55, 2.0, 3.95 and 10, `get_speed.machine_speed` equals the `g_dwSpeed` produced by calling `UseClockMultiplier` directly in-process. *(Task 1 C++ test.)*
